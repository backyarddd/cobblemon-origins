# Changelog

Client pack changes per release, newest first. Server-side changes are announced on Discord.

## 1.1.9: Mewtwo, Shrine Fixes and Cheaper Shops

No new world needed. Shrine fixes only show in chunks you have not explored yet. Server owners: update your server with the 1.1.9 server pack. The server and every player need the same version, because Cobblemon Origins Tweaks changed.

### Mewtwo
- **Mewtwo now comes from LumyMon's Ancient DNA.** Beat the Elite Four and the final boss of a Radical Cobblemon Trainers series to get one Ancient DNA: Champion Cynthia (Brilliant Diamond/Shining Pearl), Champion Terry (Radical Red) or Aklove (Unbound). You can earn one per series
- **New recipe: Cloning Catalyst.** 4 Nether Stars in the corners, 4 Psychic Gem Blocks on the sides and a Netherite Block in the middle
- Put the Ancient DNA and a Cloning Catalyst in a Restoration Tank to revive Mewtwo. Ancient DNA can also be crafted into an Origin Fossil for Mew, so each one goes to either Mewtwo or Mew
- Ancient DNA can no longer be crafted
- The old Mewtwo DNA item, its Gym Leader rewards and its Nether Star recipe are gone

### Legendary Shrines
- **Lava now flows** in the Primal Throne and the Phoenix Caldera instead of sitting still
- **All 8 Geostones in the Primal Throne can be reached.** Six now sit on the rock spires along the walkway and two in the tunnel walls. Before, two were sealed inside the rock
- Altars and everything you need to reach are easier to find: more light at the Skybreak Spire, Rimefrost Rotunda, Ironclad Mausoleum, Sundered Star and Phoenix Caldera altars, steps up from the sea at the Crescent Sanctuary jetty, lanterns at the Soul Dew Gardens landing, and a clear path through the Tree of Beginning
- The Ironclad Mausoleum's two staircases now meet the floor instead of stopping one block short

### Quest Book
- The Mewtwo quests (Legends and Myths, Legendary Encyclopedia, Legendary Shrines) explain the new Ancient DNA path. Existing quest progress is kept
- Poké Mart prices are 10% lower, matching the server shops

### Changes
- **Shops are 10% cheaper:** the Poké Mart quests and the Cobble Merchant
- **Chat Heads:** player heads no longer disappear from chat after someone whispers to you
- **Cobblemon Origins Tweaks 1.0.3** (removes the Mewtwo DNA item)

### On the Cobblemon Origins server
- **New rank ladder:** Ace, Challenger, Veteran, Elite and Champion, each in tiers I, II and III, then Master at 750 hours of active playtime. Every rank adds homes and land claims and pays CobbleDollars, with a crate key at each new rank. Existing players were placed by their hours and got the rewards for every rank up to theirs
- **New rank badges** for every tier, and a Master badge in Master Ball colours. League Champion now shows as its own badge next to your rank. Paid rank badges are hidden for now
- **`/shop buy <item> [amount]`:** buy without opening the menu, e.g. `/shop buy oak_log 64`. Short names, plurals and nicknames work (`cobble`, `obby`, `pokeball`, `ultra`, `lefties`, `max_pot`)
- **The shop has 1,100+ items:** a new Colored Blocks category with every colour of wool, carpet, concrete, terracotta and glass, all wood types' slabs, stairs, fences, doors and signs, copper and stone variants, beds, banners, candles, leaves and crafting materials. Every price is 10% lower
- **The shop's Back button** returns you to the page you came from
- **New `/msg` and `/r`:** private messages look better, the name is clickable to reply, and `/r` (or `/reply`) answers your last conversation. `/tell`, `/w` and `/whisper` work the same way

## 1.1.8: Legendary Shrines

No new world needed, but the shrines only generate in chunks you have not explored yet. Server owners: update your server with the 1.1.8 server pack. The server and every player need the same version: LumyMon runs on both sides.

### 16 Legendary Shrines
Sixteen new structures now generate across the world. Each one holds an altar that summons its Legendary or Mythical Pokémon when you use the right item on it.

- **The Primal Throne** (Groudon): a volcano island in warm oceans, crowned by six claw spires, with lava rivers, a magma chamber and the throne in its crater
- **Abyssal Sanctum** (Kyogre): a sunken temple in deep oceans. Swim in and climb up into its dry altar chamber
- **Skybreak Spire** (Rayquaza): a 140-block tower wrapped by a copper serpent, its head rearing over the summit altar
- **The Sundered Star** (Deoxys): a split meteor floating over the outer End islands, a crystal core hanging in the cleft
- **Crescent Sanctuary** (Cresselia): an island with a standing crescent moon and floating amethyst halos, in cold oceans
- **Frostwing Spire** (Articuno), **Stormcaller Citadel** (Zapdos) and **Phoenix Caldera** (Moltres): the bird altars lift you into a sky arena high above the shrine, where the bird appears. Each arena has a safe drop back down
- **Dunebound Sepulcher** (Regirock), **Rimefrost Rotunda** (Regice) and **Ironclad Mausoleum** (Registeel): three different tombs in deserts, snowy plains and dark forests
- **The Fractured Seal** (Regieleki or Regidrago): two altars face each other across a glowing crack. Awaken one and the other altar crumbles, so choose carefully
- **Tree of Beginning** (Mew): a jungle pyramid with a giant tree growing over it
- **Starfall Observatory** (Jirachi): a quartz observatory under two floating rings in meadows and flower forests
- **Bloomveil Sanctuary** (Shaymin): a flower-domed rotunda on a lukewarm-ocean island
- **Soul Dew Gardens** (Latias and Latios): a floating walled garden over the ocean with red and blue bell towers

Shrines spawn away from villages and other big structures, sit level with the terrain, and only appear in matching biomes.

### New mod: LumyMon
- Adds the shrine altars and their summon items: feathers for the birds, the Ocean, Earth and Sky Cores, relics for the Regis, the Soul Dews, the Melodic Tape, the Meteorite Crystal, the Dream Catcher and more
- The Regi relics come from LumyMon's ores. Draco Relics (Regidrago) drop from 1 in 4 Dragon Ores
- Every summon item has a crafting recipe in this pack. They are late-game: most need a Nether Star, a Heart of the Sea or an Orb. The Earth Core needs Molten Crust, made from the 8 Geostones hidden in the Primal Throne
- **Shrine radars:** craft a Locator Chip into a Poké Radar, add the shrine's item, then put the radar and an empty map in a Kanto Cartography Table for a map to the nearest shrine. The Sundered Star radar needs the End Cartography Table, used in the End
- LumyMon's remote PC (open your PC from anywhere) is turned off, and its free Raid Den cheers are off (leaving a raid also deleted every cheer in your inventory)
- LumyMon's two keybinds (dismount and remote PC) ship unbound, so they don't clash with voice chat on V and social interactions on P. Sneak still dismounts

### Quest Book
- **New chapter: Legendary Shrines** (61 quests). Find each shrine, craft its summon item and catch its Legendary
- The Kyogre, Groudon and Rayquaza quests moved from Legends and Myths into the new chapter
- The Legendary Encyclopedia now names the shrine for each of the 18 shrine Legendaries
- Quests that ask for any Legendary or Mythical now also accept Cresselia, Koraidon, Miraidon, Celebi, Jirachi, Magearna, Manaphy, Phione, Pecharunt and Zeraora
- Structure Hunter no longer starts with the Explorer's Compass; the compass is now an optional side quest
- 46 chapters and 2,093 quests in total

### Changes
- **The Explorer's Compass is much harder to craft:** a Compass in the middle, 4 Nether Stars and 4 Diamond Blocks
- **Koraidon and Miraidon now spawn in the wild** (very rare, Lv. 68-72). Look for Koraidon in jungles and highlands, and Miraidon in mountains and the Deep Dark. **Type: Null** now spawns in dark forests and the Deep Dark (Lv. 40) and evolves into Silvally with high friendship. The Legendary Encyclopedia is updated to match

## 1.1.5: Official Server, Village Treasure and Working Gacha Coins

No new world needed. Update your server with the 1.1.5 server pack.

### Multiplayer
- **New: Join Official Server button.** The big gold button at the top of the main menu takes you straight to the official Cobblemon Origins server
- **The official server is now in your Multiplayer list.** Look for **Cobblemon Origins • Official Server** (play.cobbleorigins.net) at the bottom of the list
- Your own servers are kept. The official entry is only added when it is missing, so updating never wipes your list
- The server's resource pack (rank badges and menus) is accepted automatically for the official server

### Village Loot
- **Every chest, barrel and pot in Poké Center villages now holds Pokémon loot** on top of what was there: Poké Balls, medicine, EXP candy, berries, evolution stones, vitamins, held items, mints and more
- **Some chests hold Relic Coins** to sell for CobbleDollars
- **Jackpot (very rare):** a tiny chance of a legendary-tier find, including a **Master Ball**, Ancient Origin Ball, legendary summoning items from Legendary Monuments (Azure Flute, Red Chain, Vortex Stone, Rainbow Feather, Light and Dark Stones, legendary treats and more), Mewtwonite X and Y, the Red and Blue Orbs, Shiny Card, Cobblemax and Master Candy
- Chests you already opened keep what they had. Unopened chests in villages you already found get the new loot

### Gacha and Bank
- **Gacha Coins now work.** Every gacha machine that takes Relic Coins also takes any Gacha Coin, one coin per pull
- **The Cobble Merchant bank buys every Gacha Coin for 50 CobbleDollars**
- Quest book notes updated to match

### Fixes
- The pack no longer ships leftover single-player Inventory Profiles Next files from the developer's test world

## 1.1.4: Discord Rich Presence

No new world needed.

### Discord
- **New: Discord Rich Presence.** Your Discord status now shows "Playing Cobblemon Origins" with the pack logo and your Minecraft head, plus what you are up to:
  - In the main menu, picking a server, joining a world or paused
  - Single player: your world's name and the biome you are exploring
  - Online: the server name, player count and your biome
  - The Nether and the End get their own lines
- Friends viewing your status get **Get the Modpack** and **Join the Discord** buttons
- Don't want it? Turn it off with `enabled = false` in `config/simple-rpc/simple-rpc.toml`

### Economy
- **The Cobble Merchant bank no longer buys Emeralds or Emerald Blocks.** Villager trades (especially after curing a zombie villager) turned shop goods into endless CobbleDollars. The bank still buys Relic Coins (50), Pouches (475) and Sacks (4,500)
- Quest book notes updated to match, with a new "Bank Holiday" note in the CobbleDollars chapter

## 1.1.3: Backpack Slot and Clean Keybinds

No new world needed.

### Backpacks
- **New: a Back slot for your backpack.** Open your inventory, click the Accessories button next to your character and put any Sophisticated Backpack in the Back slot. Open it from there with B

### Keybinds
- **Tab only opens the player list.** The quest book's "next chapter" key no longer takes it over
- **No more clashing keys.** Several keys were shared by two actions, so one of them silently did nothing. Every key now does exactly one thing:
  - F (swap offhand), Space (jump), P (social), T (chat), Left Shift (sneak) and middle-click (pick block) are no longer shared with the quest book, Trash Slot, Legendary Tooltips or backpack sorting
  - Z zooms (Zoomify)
  - Quest book: scroll to zoom and drag to move the view; player settings are on the button in the book
  - Backpacks and storage: sort with the sort button in their screens
  - Scroll long tooltips: Right Shift
  - Show or hide the Trash Slot: End
  - Xaero's maps: Home enlarges the minimap, F7 hides or shows it, Page Up and Page Down zoom the world map, Right Ctrl is quick confirm, F6 opens minimap settings, = and - zoom the minimap

### Gameplay
- **New players get a Trainer Card**, placed in the offhand after the Quest Book
- **Ultimine breaks up to 16 blocks at once** (was 64)

### Fixes
- Inventory Profiles Next no longer lights up every slot of a server menu button green

### Updating an existing install
- Keys you set yourself are kept. If something still clashes, Options > Controls > Key Binds shows it in red; set it to the key listed above
- The Trainer Card goes to players who have not claimed the Welcome quest yet
